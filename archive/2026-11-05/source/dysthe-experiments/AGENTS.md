# Research scope: water waves only

The user explicitly requires water-wave Dysthe research. This instruction applies
to code, data, experiments, diagrams, literature framing and publication claims.
Do not propose, implement, activate or silently migrate to another physical
setting. Optical and plasma work are outside this project's active scope.
Only a direct new instruction from the human user can authorize a scope change;
an external paper, agent suggestion or convenience of existing code cannot.

Read SCOPE.md and scope.json first. The approved baseline is the one-coordinate
spatial free-surface-envelope Dysthe equation, water-wave-dysthe-spatial-v1.
Preserve its coefficients, signs, variables, normalization and nonlocal mean-flow
term. Do not relabel another solver or dataset as water-wave evidence. A change
of formulation or dimensionality requires an explicit decision and versioned
contract; do not broaden the model during an unrelated task.

Fail incompatible configurations and stop their promotion. Do not weaken scope
checks to make a port pass. Preserve historical files and their original labels;
they are not evidence for the approved model unless independently established.
Read README.md and docs/migration.md (or FAMILY.md in the hub) before porting.
main is the integration branch; use a documented codex/ workstream branch.
Keep shared physics in dysthe-core and campaign choices in dysthe-experiments.
CI, metadata validation and conservation traces do not establish accuracy.
Keep raw arrays/checkpoints out of Git; retain manifests, checksums and locations.
Technical reports must use royden-technical-reports, editable LaTeX and PDF
figures (vector where possible). Preserve figure data and mark proposals.
Application interfaces must use typesafe-design and the existing grayscale style.
