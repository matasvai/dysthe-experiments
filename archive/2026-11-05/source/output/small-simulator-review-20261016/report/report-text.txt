1         Problem and prescribed inputs
The simulator advances the scaled complex free-surface envelope u(ξ, τ ), where ξ is propagation
distance and τ retarded time:

                               uξ = −iuτ τ − i|u|2 u − ϵ 8|u|2 uτ + 2u2 uτ + 2iu|Dτ |(|u|2 ) .
                                                                                                                    


The model contract is water-wave-dysthe-spatial-v1. The coefficients are (1, 1, 8, 2, 2). The
periodic interval is [−80, 80), N = 4096, ϵ = 0.025, ∆ξ = 0.00125, and 0 ≤ ξ ≤ 4. There are 81
saved positions. The Fourier method retains |m| < N/4, uses mean-flow symbol |k| with zero mean
mode, and applies Lawson integrating-factor RK4.
   Let F be the recomputed periodic branch with µ = 2, s = 0, and full traveling residual 2.03×10−11 .
The three initial fields are F (τ ); F (τ )[1+0.02 exp(−((τ −1.3)/2)2 )]; and exp(−τ 2 /8) exp(0.3iτ ). The
second is a shifted localized amplitude perturbation. The third has amplitude 1, width parameter 2,
wavenumber 0.3, and zero chirp. The plots retain physical coordinates and show envelope amplitude,
rather than reconstructed surface elevation.

3    Numerical qualification and reproducibility
Each default case has independent comparisons with N = 8192 at fixed domain, ∆ξ = 0.000625 at
fixed grid, and (L, N ) = (320, 8192) at fixed spacing. All nine comparisons and the recorded design
screens pass. Profiles are recomputed for each period. The table reports maxima over all 81 saved
positions, retaining complex phase in the raw comparisons.

    The raw-field normalization is the refined field norm at each position. The chosen gates are
2×10−4 for raw differences and 10−4 for orbital differences and signed-shape discrepancies. The latter
compare each solution’s deformation from its own initial field. Their additional relative response
gate is 0.02, used only above a 10−7 field-norm response floor. The tiny reference response falls
below that floor. Two levels demonstrate agreement; they provide neither an observed convergence
order nor a rigorous error bound. Periodic mean flow can depend on L even when boundary action
is tiny; domain differences include that dependence.
    Signed-shape comparisons can have an approximately 10−8 fitting floor under the inherited
orbital optimizer even when raw grid differences are near 10−15 . This is a diagnostic precision limit,
not evidence of an unresolved propagated field. Action conservation is supporting evidence; padded
continuum energy and momentum are not assumed exact invariants of the projected equation or
RK map.
    The stored arrays tau, xi, u, and initial are available in each case’s fields.npz; u is complex128
of shape (81, 4096). Reports record parameters, definitions, qualification, source hashes, and field
checksums. Plotting verifies those checksums and metadata against the saved summary before
reading the fields. The completed 12-trajectory campaign, including refinements, used 106.3 seconds
of solver/diagnostic time and peak process RSS 197.0 MiB.
